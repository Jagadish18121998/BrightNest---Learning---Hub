💡 Key Mathematical Ideas

  • Every number has a definite position on the number line.
  • Moving to the right represents increasing values.
  • Moving to the left represents decreasing values.

👩‍🏫 Teacher's Demonstration Tips

  • Ask learners to predict the final position before creating a jump.
  • Invite learners to explain the movement shown by the curved arc.
  • Connect the visual movement with the corresponding mathematical expression.
