👩‍🏫 Teacher Toolkit

Turn on Teacher Mode for classroom prompts and pronunciation reminders.

Suggested classroom routine

  1. Display the selected letter and say its name.
  2. Model the common sound clearly. Avoid adding an extra vowel to a consonant sound.
  3. Demonstrate the mouth position. Invite children to observe in a mirror.
  4. Say the five example words and ask learners to identify the shared sound.
  5. Ask children to suggest another word. Accept suitable words and discuss different sounds when relevant.

Support for learners who need more practice

  • Begin with just two contrasting sounds.
  • Use a mirror and picture cards.
  • Repeat a short listen–say–identify routine.
  • Move from recognising a sound to saying it, then to finding it in words.

Pronunciation reminder: These guides are simplified classroom cues. English sounds vary by accent, and a written letter does not always represent the same sound.
