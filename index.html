ABOUT BRIGHTNEST

A Little Nest for Big Ideas

BrightNest Learning Hub is a digital learning initiative of PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya Mahe.

It brings learning resources, classroom support, interactive activities and practice tools into one accessible space.

The platform is designed to support both learners and teachers while keeping learning simple, engaging and joyful.
