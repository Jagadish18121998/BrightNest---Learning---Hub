🌱 A Small Team

Four children were preparing a garden corner for their classroom. Tara brought seeds, Aman made small labels, Nisha watered the plants and Kabir arranged the pots. When one pot fell over, nobody became angry. They cleaned the soil and placed the pot carefully again. They discovered that each person could do something useful. By working peacefully, they finished the garden corner before the lunch bell rang. Their teacher told them that a successful team is not one where everyone does the same thing. It is one where everyone contributes towards the same goal.
